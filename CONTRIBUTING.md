# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this campaign website launch checklist.

## Good contributions

- identify a concrete, repeatable launch check
- explain the visitor or campaign-team problem it prevents
- provide a simple verification method
- use neutral language that works across parties, offices, and jurisdictions
- cite primary sources for standards or jurisdiction-specific requirements

## Please avoid

- partisan messaging or persuasion tactics
- endorsements of candidates, parties, or vendors
- keyword-stuffed promotional copy
- claims that a check guarantees legal compliance or search rankings
- personal data, voter data, credentials, or confidential campaign information

## Suggesting a change

Open an issue with:

1. the section where the check belongs
2. the proposed wording
3. its suggested severity
4. the reason it matters
5. a verification method

By contributing, you agree that your submission may be incorporated into this public resource.
