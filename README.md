# Political Campaign Website Launch Checklist

An open, nonpartisan checklist for candidates and campaign teams preparing a campaign website for launch.

**[Use the interactive checklist](https://pabloespinal.github.io/political-campaign-website-launch-checklist/)**

The checklist covers:

- candidate, office, district, and election-date clarity
- issue pages, biography, endorsements, events, and news
- volunteer, contact, and external donation paths
- mobile usability, accessibility, performance, and security
- English/Spanish and multilingual review
- search previews, structured data, analytics, and launch QA
- disclaimer and compliance-review reminders
- post-election archiving and transition planning

## Why this exists

Campaign websites are assembled under deadlines, often by small teams. This resource turns launch readiness into a repeatable review instead of an informal final check.

It is deliberately platform-neutral. You can use it with a custom-built site, a general website builder, or a campaign-specific platform.

## Ways to use it

1. Open the [interactive checklist](https://pabloespinal.github.io/political-campaign-website-launch-checklist/) and work through each section.
2. Import [`data/checklist.csv`](data/checklist.csv) into a spreadsheet or project-management tool.
3. Read [`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md) to understand how the checks are organized.
4. Open an issue to suggest a missing check or share field experience.

Checkbox progress is stored only in your browser. The page has no analytics, advertising, account system, or form submission.
